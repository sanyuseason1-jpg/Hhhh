Help & Support

How CAPITALLOOP Works

1. Recharge: Submit a mobile money recharge request. Once approved, funds enter your Available Recharge Balance.

2. Buy & Hire: Select any confirmed package and choose a 12-hour or 24-hour hiring cycle.

3. Complete & Receive: When the active hiring period ends, the agreed return is credited to your Withdrawable Balance.

4. Withdraw: Request payouts of any amount from your Withdrawable Balance (5% withdrawal tax applies).
